THIRD-PARTY IDENTITY COMPROMISE & ACCESS GOVERNANCE LAB
Case ID: TPI-2026-001

INCIDENT
Third-party vendor account: jcarter_vendor
Vendor: Apex Support Solutions
Affected Asset: Finance-Server

FINDING
The vendor account was approved for standard application support access and was not authorized for privileged administrative access.

INVESTIGATION
Activity analysis identified a denied Finance-Server access attempt followed by successful privileged activity. The account created temp_admin and added the account to the Server-Administrators group, violating the approved vendor access profile.

RISK ASSESSMENT
Impact: 5
Likelihood: 4
Risk Score: 20
Risk Rating: Critical

REMEDIATION
Unauthorized Server-Administrators access was removed.
Least-privilege controls were restored.
Additional MFA and privileged-access monitoring were recommended.

VALIDATION
PASS - No unauthorized privileged access remains after remediation.

INCIDENT REPORT - THIRD-PARTY IDENTITY SECURITY

Case ID: TPI-2026-001
Third Party: Apex Support Solutions
Vendor Account: jcarter_vendor
Affected Asset: Finance-Server

FINDING
Unauthorized Third-Party Privilege Escalation

INVESTIGATION SUMMARY
Vendor account activity was reviewed against the approved access baseline.
The account was authorized for standard application support access and was
not approved for privileged administrative access.

Investigation identified successful privileged activity involving the
Finance-Server and Server-Administrators group, inconsistent with the
vendor's approved access profile.

RISK ASSESSMENT
Impact: 5
Likelihood: 4
Risk Score: 20
Risk Rating: Critical

REMEDIATION
Unauthorized Server-Administrators access was removed.
Least-privilege controls were restored.
Additional MFA and privileged-access monitoring were recommended.

VALIDATION
PASS - No unauthorized privileged access remains after remediation.
